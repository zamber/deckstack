## What this means for the new base

The two implementations agree on the hardware contract and diverge on everything above it. The new
base takes the Rust-side wins that are data/protocol (safety-by-construction, pipelined writes,
latest-frame-wins queue, guard handback, loopback token API) and the Python-side wins that are
operational (virtual-hub conformance harness, Linux platform seam, richer external control
surface), and discards both walled gardens: the hardcoded action catalogs, the in-process plugin
default, the GUI-coupled engine, and the ephemeral-port token-file discovery.
