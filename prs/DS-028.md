## Risks

The three goldens are recorded fixtures with `skip_reason`, not strict checks, because
this M4315 unit keeps the last pushed colour per key index and ignores the LED `off`
mode, so the same state does not reproduce minutes apart. `--strict` turns every skip
into a failure, and the follow-up ticket owns making the device state fully specified.

## Findings

Measured 2026-11-24 on the reference pose (panel rect 395,148,600,495, orientation 90):
key0 matched 17/17 targets immediately after recording, then failed on the untouched
keys 1-14 and `led:0`; panel failed 15/17 with the lit keys in different positions
(recorded key:3 rgb(126,164,220) vs check rgb(254,251,244)); led failed 3/17. The LED
rings hold their last colour across daemon restarts, so a fresh daemon data dir does
not mean a fresh device. Goldens stay test fixtures only (empty-state invariant).
