## Findings

- systemd's `ExecStart=` expansion has no shell-style `${VAR:-default}` (unset variables
  expand to empty), so the unit supplies the device default via
  `Environment=DECKSTACK_DEVICE=none` and lets `EnvironmentFile=-%h/.config/deckstack/env`
  override it — the systemd-native equivalent of `${DECKSTACK_DEVICE:-none}`.
- The unit deliberately avoids `PrivateTmp`/`PrivateDevices` so `/tmp` vdev sockets and
  phase-2 `/dev/hidraw*` stay reachable.
